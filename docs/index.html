Android inventory companion

Near Expiry

Near Expiry helps stores and teams scan products, track quantities and expiry dates, run Stock Rechecks, and prepare practical inventory exports.

Read the Privacy Policy View the source project

Expiry management

Track products and receive staged local notifications for products that are expired, expire today, or are approaching expiry.

Stock Recheck

Load a Stock Recheck Excel file, scan matched items, record physical quantities, and preserve the source workbook format for export.

Optional Drive backup

Users may connect their own Google account to back up app-created JSON backup files in a visible Near Expiry Backups folder in their own Google Drive.
