Near Expiry Privacy Policy

Near Expiry is an Android inventory and expiry-management application. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use its local features and optional Google Drive backup.

Return to the Near Expiry homepage

1. Information stored on your device

Near Expiry stores the inventory information you enter or import on your device. This can include project names, product codes, descriptions, quantities, expiry dates, locally selected catalog files, and Stock Recheck Excel reference data. The app uses this information to provide scanning, expiry tracking, history, notifications, and export features.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional. It is used only after you choose to connect a Google account and grant permission. When enabled, Near Expiry creates JSON backup files in a visible folder named Near Expiry Backups in the Google Drive account you selected.

The app requests the limited Google Drive drive.file permission so it can create, upload, list, download, and delete the backup files and folder it manages for this feature. The app does not use a developer-owned server to receive or store your inventory backups. Google Play services manages Google account authorization; the app does not store your Google OAuth access or refresh tokens itself.

3. Backup retention and your choices

The app retains recent automatic backups according to its in-app backup settings. You can disconnect Google Drive, switch the connected account, delete app-created Drive backup files from Google Drive, or delete local backups using your device’s file management tools. Disconnecting Google Drive does not delete files already stored in your Google Drive account.

4. Network and local device features

The app may use the network only for features you initiate or enable, including optional Google Drive backup, checking for app updates, and optional local-network catalog transfer with a compatible PC. Near Expiry notifications and inventory calculations are performed on your device.

5. Sharing of information

Near Expiry does not sell your inventory information. Your optional Google Drive backups are sent directly to the Google account you choose, subject to Google’s own terms and privacy practices. You control that Google account and its files.

6. Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and Google account. Use a device screen lock and secure your Google account with Google’s available security settings. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Near Expiry features or applicable requirements change. The current version will be available at this page.
